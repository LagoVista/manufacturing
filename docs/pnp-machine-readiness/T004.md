## T004.1 — Opt-in geometry diagnostic

Checkbox in the existing calibration dialog. Preview the configured diagonal
references, stagger-aware cross references, and return to the first hole.
Confirm all intended holes are visible and unobstructed before Run. The
calibrated machine fiducial anchors nominal coordinates; do not treat it as a
staging hole. Record nominal, initial and centered coordinates and the first
qualified optical correction. Fit three points, check the fourth independently,
report axis scale, angle, cross terms and return difference. No saved calibration,
automatic rehoming, changed motion limits or machine-wide compensation. Existing
safe motion, centering limits and fresh observations remain authoritative.
Repeat manually after homing or power cycling. Two supervised diagnostic runs completed on the development machine; production-machine validation is deferred.

The fitted axis angle changed by +0.2386 degrees. Across the 300 mm nominal
X span, the change in right-minus-left Y was +1.100 mm on row C and +0.900 mm
on row Q. The pattern is consistent with a change in gantry squareness after
motor release and rehoming, but this combined experiment does not isolate
motor release from homing or optical measurement uncertainty. Fixed plate
manufacturing error cannot by itself explain a change between runs.
The zero reported return differences indicate same-run repeatability at
C3 within the measurement resolution and centering tolerance, not zero
physical error throughout travel. The significant holdout residuals and
uncertified plate geometry preclude trusting a static affine correction
at the required placement accuracy.

## Accepted decision and deferred follow-up

- Retain the opt-in, non-persisting geometry diagnostic as a permanent tool.
- No automatic compensation, firmware changes, motor-control changes, or
  mechanical redesign authorized. Preserve existing calibration and safety limits.
- Avoid unnecessary manual motion with the motors released; use normal
  safe homing and machine-fiducial calibration following manual repositioning.
- Revisit on the production machine using repeat runs with motors energized,
  rehoming alone, and a separate release/reposition/rehoming experiment.
  Use an independently verified reference if absolute geometric accuracy is
  required. Compare the relative left/right errors before choosing a correction.
- Evaluate a second homing switch and independently homed motors only if the
  production-machine results justify it and the motion controller supports it.
- Operator accepted closure of the investigation on November 27, 2026.
  T004 is not a blocker to continued readiness work. This closure does not
  certify production-machine squareness or 0.2 mm placement accuracy.
